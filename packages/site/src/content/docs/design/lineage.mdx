---

coeval takes each of its ideas from earlier work. This page names the sources.

| Idea of coeval | Source |
| --- | --- |
| An evaluator gets its parts as documents | John Shutt, [the Kernel language](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~jshutt/kernel.html) and [the thesis of Shutt on fexprs](https://dcreager.net/papers/Shutt2010/): operatives get their operands without evaluation |
| An evaluator must not look inside its parts | Mitchell Wand, "The Theory of Fexprs is Trivial" (1998). A summary is in [the article on fexprs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fexpr). |
| Finite syntax, and meaning by observation | Turi and Plotkin, [Towards a Mathematical Operational Semantics](https://www.dcs.ed.ac.uk/home/dt/towards.html) (1997): syntax is an algebra, behavior is a coalgebra |
| A program for the syntax, an interpreter for the behavior | The pair of a free monad and a cofree comonad, in [this series on interpreters](https://github.com/dalaing/cofun/blob/master/blog/posts/free_and_cofree.md) |
| Data that goes down and up a tree | Donald Knuth, [Semantics of Context-Free Languages](https://www.doi.org/10.1007/BF01692511) (1968): inherited and synthesized attributes |
| References sideways, and equations from an ancestor | Görel Hedin, reference attribute grammars, in [JastAdd](https://jastadd.cs.lth.se/web/documentation/concept-overview.php) |
| A formula at a position, and dataflow | Uustalu and Vene, [the comonadic view of dataflow and attribute grammars](https://mail.haskell.org/pipermail/haskell/2005-September/016505.html) |
| Constraints go down, sizes go up | [The layout of Flutter](https://docs.flutter.dev/ui/layout/constraints) |
| A schedule is separate from the task | Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones, [Build Systems à la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/build-systems-la-carte/) (2018) |
| Names for the results of earlier steps | The paths of [the states of AWS Step Functions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/dg/input-output-example.html), and the outputs of the steps of GitHub Actions |
| New kinds against new operations | William Cook, [On Understanding Data Abstraction, Revisited](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~wcook/Drafts/2009/essay.pdf) (2009) |
| A kind with aspects | The concepts and aspects of [JetBrains MPS](https://www.jetbrains.com/help/mps/mps-project-structure.html) |
